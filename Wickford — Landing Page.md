The problem

## Missed calls aren't lost customers. They're customers who called someone else.

When you're mid-job, calls go to voicemail. Most people don't leave one. They hang up and dial the next name on the list. By the time you call back, the job's already booked with a competitor.

**Ringing, unanswered**

A call comes in while you're up a ladder or under a sink. It rings out.

**No callback**

Most callers won't leave a voicemail, and by evening the details are cold.

**Job goes elsewhere**

They call the next number on their search. You never even knew you lost it.
